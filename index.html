The Challenge

Funding is out there.
Finding it is the problem.

Most TK-12 districts are leaving money on the table — not because they don't qualify, but because they don't have the time, staff, or systems to pursue it consistently.

01

Too Little Time

Administrators and grant coordinators are already stretched thin. Researching grants across hundreds of sources takes hours districts simply don't have.

02

Not Enough Staff

Many districts have one part-time grant writer — or none at all. Smaller districts compete against well-resourced applicants with dedicated development offices.

03

No Central Source

Grant opportunities are scattered across government portals, foundations, and newsletters. There's no single place to find what your district actually qualifies for.

Trust & Safety

Built responsibly, for people who manage public funds.

The Money Website is designed with the realities of school district work in mind — data sensitivity, budget accountability, and the need for every application to hold up to scrutiny.

🔒

Data Privacy

Your district's information — enrollment data, demographics, and funding goals — is used only to improve grant matching accuracy and personalize draft recommendations. We do not sell or share district data with third parties.

👀

Human Review

AI-generated grant drafts are a starting point, not a final product. Every draft should be reviewed, fact-checked, and approved by district staff — including your grant coordinator or finance office — before submission.

✅

Verified Grant Sources

Every grant listed includes a link to its official source, current deadline, eligibility requirements, and a last-updated date, so your team can confirm details directly with the funding agency before applying.

⚖️

No Funding Guarantee

The Money Website is designed to improve efficiency and application quality, helping your team find more relevant opportunities and submit stronger applications. It does not guarantee that any application will be funded.

🏫

Built for TK-12

This platform is built specifically for the people who manage school district funding — superintendents, finance officers, grant coordinators, and administrators — not adapted from a general-purpose business tool.
